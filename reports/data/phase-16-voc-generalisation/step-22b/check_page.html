Step 22b: which scan carries the folio?

Each item is a Generale Missiven anchor (folio N at a scan) where the folio map disagreed with the gold and the printed numbers near the gold scan suggested the gold itself might be off. For every item, open the scans and decide which scan carries folio N. Each item shows image crops of the top of the page (where VOC folio numbers are written) for the scan before the gold, the gold scan (yellow frame), the scan after it and the scan the map predicted; crops come from the Nationaal Archief image server and need an internet connection. If the number sits lower on the page, use larger or the scan link. The fold-out lists the page numbers the text layer read on nearby scans (gold row yellow). Choose gold scan when the gold is right, predicted scan when the map is right, another scan (and type its number), or cannot tell. The result only says how much of the miss rate is gold noise; it blocks nothing. When done, press Export and save check_verdicts.json in reports/data/phase-16-voc-generalisation/step-22b/.
