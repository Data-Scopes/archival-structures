Step 22b: which scan carries the folio?

Each item is a Generale Missiven anchor (folio N at a scan) where the folio map disagreed with the gold and the printed numbers near the gold scan suggested the gold itself might be off. For every item, open the scans and decide which scan carries folio N. The table shows the page numbers the text layer read on the scans around the gold scan (yellow) and around the scan the map predicted. Choose gold scan when the gold is right, predicted scan when the map is right, another scan (and type its number), or cannot tell. The result only says how much of the miss rate is gold noise; it blocks nothing. When done, press Export and save check_verdicts.json in reports/data/phase-16-voc-generalisation/step-22b/.
