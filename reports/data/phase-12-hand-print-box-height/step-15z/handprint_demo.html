Hand or print demo

Instructions

Demo built from the step 15z items: 37 volumes x 5 scans. Judge each crop; press a key to store and advance.

Handwritten (H): written by hand. Cues: ragged lines of uneven length, abbreviations such as Reg:, miss:, marginal dates, corrections and crossings-out.
Printed (P): typeset roman type. Cues: regular lines of similar length, no corrections, printed page numbers or signatures such as ( 83 ) or A 2, catchwords at the foot of the page, running heads.
Printed, Gothic (G): printed in Gothic (blackletter) type. It may lower OCR quality, so flag it separately.
Mixed page (M): both hand and print on this page (for example a printed form filled in by hand). Unsure (U): you cannot tell. No text (X): blank, cover, image or otherwise not a text page.
