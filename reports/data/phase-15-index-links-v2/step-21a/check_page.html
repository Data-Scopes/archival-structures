Check 20 index-link targets (phase 15, step 21a)

This page shows the agent's answers: it is a confirmation, not a blind test. For each index entry of a States General register (1.01.02), the agent read the folio number the entry ends in (the first locator), found that folio in the volume, and judged whether the entry's matter is there. The 20 items are drawn at random (seed 20261031) from the 63 test targets with a scan and a printed or counted folio number.

Folios are numbered on the right-hand page (recto) only; the back of folio N is the left-hand page of the next scan. In each item open the first scan (right-hand page: is the printed number N, or does counting from nearby printed numbers give N?) and the second scan (left-hand page). Answer Yes / No / Unsure on all three questions; use the note for anything odd (for example the true folio, or that the entry text above mixes two entries). The entry text is the HTR of the entry's lines joined in reading order, lines separated by " / "; when a line was merged with the other column, that column's words may show up in the text.

Each item shows image crops from the Nationaal Archief image server, each with the text the layer extracted for those lines below it: the entry's lines, the top of the target right-hand page (the folio number), and the lines where the agent found the matter (matched automatically from the agent's evidence, on the sides the agent named; up to three crops). They are for convenience; when a crop is cut off or wrong, open the scan link. Crops need an internet connection.

The fold-outs show the extracted text of the two target pages in reading order. Margin notes are marked [margin] and placed at their height on the page; a dashed rule separates text columns; the lines of the evidence crops are highlighted. It is handwriting recognition, so expect errors; the scan link is the authority.

When done, press Export and save the file as reports/data/phase-15-index-links-v2/step-21a/check_verdicts.json (AS root). Answers are kept in this browser until then.
