Check 20 index links (phase 14, step 20c)

This page shows the agent's answer: it is a confirmation, not a blind test. For each index entry of a States General register (1.01.02), the agent read the folio number the entry ends in, found that folio in the volume, and judged whether the entry's matter is there.

Folios are numbered on the right-hand page (recto) only; the back of folio N is the left-hand page of the next scan. In each item open the first scan (right-hand page: is the printed number N, or does counting from nearby printed numbers give N?) and the second scan (left-hand page). Answer Yes / No / Unsure on both questions; use the note for anything odd (for example the true folio).

Each item shows three image crops from the Nationaal Archief image server, each with the text the layer extracted for those lines below it: the entry, the top of the target right-hand page (the folio number), and the lines where the agent found the matter. They are for convenience; when a crop is cut off or wrong, open the scan link. Crops need an internet connection.

For question 2, two fold-outs under the crops show the full extracted text of the right-hand page of the first scan and the left-hand page of the second scan, in reading order. Margin notes are marked [margin] and placed at their height on the page; a dashed rule separates text columns; the lines of the evidence crop are highlighted. The text is the layer's handwriting recognition, so expect errors.

When done, press Export and save the file as reports/data/phase-14-index-links/step-20c/check_verdicts.json (AS root). Answers are kept in this browser until then.
