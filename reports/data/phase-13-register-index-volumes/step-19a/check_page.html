Phase 13, step 19a: is this page side an index?

30 page sides from the 1.01.02 text layer. For each, judge only the named page side on the scan (open the link; verso = left page of the opening, recto = right page, single = the whole scan). The first lines of the side are given to help you find it. The crops come from the Nationaal Archief images; judge the whole page side (lower crop), the upper crop only locates the quoted lines. Your judgement is compared with a reading made from the text alone.

  • Index: the main content of the side is a list of entries that each point to a resolution or document, by date and/or a page/folio reference, usually under lemma headings (place, person, subject). Registers, repertoria, tafels and respecten count. Example: Reqt: P: Roos cums: ten eijnde … 41. or 17. April. Ry. voor den Consil de lon … under a heading.
  • Not index: running text (resolutions, minutes, letters, reports), lists without a reference per entry (names, accounts), title pages, near-empty pages. Minutes in a half-page column (Ontfangen een missive van …) are not index. A side that is partly index and partly running text goes by the majority.
  • Unsure: you cannot decide; say why in the note.
