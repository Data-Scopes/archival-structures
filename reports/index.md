# AS reports

There is one report per phase. `plan/` records how the work went (briefs, `PROGRESS.md`,
`STATUS.md`); `reports/` records what was found: results, error analysis and findings.
Each step session writes its own section of its phase report, and the phase review confirms
the findings. Build the HTML with `make reports` (output in `reports/_build/`). Conventions are
in `plan/README.md` and `plan/templates/REPORT.md`.

## Finding IDs

- `F<phase>-<step>.<n>`, e.g. `F02-05.2`: finding *n* of step 05 in phase 02.
- `F<phase>-P.<n>`: a planning finding. `F<phase>-R.<n>`: a finding of the phase review.
- IDs never change and are never reused. A wrong finding is marked `withdrawn`, not deleted.
- Kinds: `result`, `behaviour-change`, `bug-fixed`, `data-property`, `negative-result`,
  `open-issue`, `method`.
- Statuses: `draft` (step), `confirmed` or `revised` (review), `withdrawn`.
- Find all mentions with `grep -rn F02-05.2 reports/ plan/`.
